Blue Meridian Ops finds where your operation loses time, then builds the systems to get it back. That usually means AI automation and workflow design, though what it actually means depends on what we find when we look.

Most of the operations we see run on knowledge that lives in people's heads, which works fine until the person who knows something steps out of the room, and stops working entirely when they leave the company. Decisions get made from memory, reports get rebuilt by hand every month, and follow-ups go missing somewhere between a conversation and an inbox.

It isn't that people are careless. Nothing in the setup was built to catch any of it, so the same work gets done twice and nobody has time to notice.

We start by watching how your team actually operates, not how the process doc says it should, then build around what's already there: your tools, your people, your constraints.
